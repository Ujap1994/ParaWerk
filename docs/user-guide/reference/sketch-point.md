# Point

> **Where:** *Sketch* tab → *Draw* group → **Point** · **Key:** P · **Needs:** an open sketch

Sketch points mark positions: the centers of [holes](hole.md), reference positions for dimensions.

## Step by step

1. Press **P** or click **Point**.
2. Click to place points; on a line or circle they stay on it (*on curve*).
3. **Esc** leaves the tool.

## Tips

- [Dimension](sketch-dimension.md) points to the origin or to edges of the part to place holes exactly.
- The [Hole](hole.md) feature drills at every point that is not the end or center of a curve and not a
  construction point.
- A row of points: place one, select it and use a [linear pattern](sketch-copy-move.md).
