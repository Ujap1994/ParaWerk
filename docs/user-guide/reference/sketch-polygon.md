# Polygon

> **Where:** *Sketch* tab → *Draw* group → **Polygon** · **Needs:** an open sketch

A regular polygon: all sides equal, the corners on a construction circle – for hexagons of nuts and heads,
triangles, octagons.

## Step by step

1. Click **Polygon** and set the number of *Sides* in the side panel (3 to 64).
2. Click the center, then a corner.

## Tips

- Dimension the construction circle (the size across the corners) or, with an even number of sides, the
  distance between two opposite sides (the size across flats). Make one side horizontal (or dimension its
  angle) to fix the rotation, and dimension or relate the center.
