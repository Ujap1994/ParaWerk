# Slot

> **Where:** *Sketch* tab → *Draw* group → **Slot** · **Needs:** an open sketch

A straight slot: two equal end arcs joined by two straight sides tangent to them – for elongated holes and
adjustment slots.

## Step by step

1. Click **Slot**.
2. Click the center of one rounded end, then the center of the other.
3. Move sideways to set the width and click.

## Tips

- Dimension the distance between the two arc centers and the radius (or width) of an end arc.
- Extrude it with **Remove material** and **Through all** for a slot through a plate.
