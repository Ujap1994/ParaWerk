# View tools

> **Where:** the floating toolbar at the top of the 3D view, the view cube (top right), the *View* menu

## Toolbar

| Button | What it does | Key |
|---|---|---|
| *Zoom to fit* | Fits the whole part into the view | F |
| *Standard views* | Front, back, left, right, top, bottom, isometric | Ctrl/⌘ + 1 … 7 |
| *Toggle perspective* | Orthographic (default) or perspective projection | |
| *Section view* | Cuts the part open: see [Section view](section-view.md) | |
| *Show reference planes, axes and points* | Hides or shows reference planes, axes, points and coordinate systems; they still show while you pick references, and when selected | |
| *Show relation symbols* | In sketch mode: relation symbols on or off | |

The views follow ISO naming: *front* looks along +Y, *top* down along −Z, *right* along −X; Z points up.

## View cube

Click a face, an edge or a corner of the cube to look from that direction. Its labels follow the interface
language.

## Mouse and trackpad

| Action | Mouse preset | Trackpad preset |
|---|---|---|
| Rotate | Middle button, or Alt + left button | Two-finger scroll, or Alt + left button |
| Pan | Shift or Ctrl + middle button, or right button | Shift + two-finger scroll, or Alt + Shift + left button |
| Zoom | Wheel (towards the cursor) | Pinch (towards the cursor) |

The part rotates around the point under the cursor when you start rotating (a small orange dot marks it).
Choose the preset in the [settings](settings.md).

## Selecting

Faces and edges are the exact faces and edges of the model: hovering a cylinder highlights the whole
cylindrical face, and the status bar shows its type and size (*Face 7 · Cylinder Ø 44 mm · 4,838.1 mm²*).
Click to select, click again to deselect; **Ctrl**, **Shift** or **⌘** adds to the selection; **Esc** clears
it.
