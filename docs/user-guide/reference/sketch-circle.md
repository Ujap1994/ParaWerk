# Circle and 3-point circle

> **Where:** *Sketch* tab → *Draw* group → **Circle** (its arrow offers **3-point circle** and
> **Ellipse**) · **Key:** C · **Needs:** an open sketch

Circles for holes, bosses and round profiles.

| Variant | How to draw |
|---|---|
| **Circle** | Click the center, then a point on the circle (or drag) |
| **3-point circle** | Click three points the circle passes through |

## Step by step

1. Press **C** or choose the variant in the arrow next to **Circle**.
2. Click as in the table; the radius is shown next to the cursor.
3. Draw more circles, or press **Esc** to leave the tool.

## Tips

- [Dimension](sketch-dimension.md) a circle with one click: it gets a diameter (Ø).
- Several circles of the same size: select them and add *Equal* in the side panel, then one diameter sizes
  them all.
- Drag the edge of a circle to change its radius, its center to move it.
- For hole positions, [points](sketch-point.md) and the [Hole](hole.md) feature are usually better than
  circles: they know the standard sizes.
