# ParaWerk 0.4.0-alpha.1 – patterns of patterns and curves

A second alpha of **Phase 3** of the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md), on top of
[0.4.0-alpha.0](v0.4.0-alpha.0.md): patterns can now copy other patterns – requested right after the
first tests of 0.4 – and projected and composite curves give sweeps paths in space. It is an alpha to
try out and give feedback on, not yet a tool for productive work.

## Highlights

### Patterns of patterns

**Pattern**, **Circular pattern** and **Mirror** now also copy other patterns and mirror images – with
the original and all of its copies. Turn a row of holes around the center of a flange, copy a row of
bosses into a grid, or mirror one half of a part together with its patterns. Pick the pattern in the
feature tree or click one of its copies in the 3D view; its originals and copies are highlighted. A
feature that would land at the same place twice is made only once.

![A row of three holes turned four times around the center of a disk: a circular pattern of a linear pattern](../images/screenshots/pattern-of-pattern.png)

### Projected and composite curves

Two new curves in the arrow of the **Plane** button, both usable as the path of a **Sweep**:

- **Projected curve** puts the curves of a sketch onto faces of the part along the sketch normal – on
  both sides of the sketch or only one – or makes a 3D curve from two views (**sketch on sketch**).
- **Composite curve** joins edges of the part, sketches and other curves end to end into one chain.

| A circle projected onto a shaft | Edges and a sketch line joined into one path |
|---|---|
| ![Projected curve](../images/screenshots/curve-projected.png) | ![Composite curve](../images/screenshots/curve-composite.png) |

### Fixes

- After a clean close, ParaWerk no longer offers to restore the document at the next start (this
  happened mostly on Linux).
- A sketch opened while a part is still being built (right after opening a file) turns the view all the
  way to the sketch plane.

## Which file do I need?

| System | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (Intel/AMD) | `ParaWerk-0.4.0-alpha.1-win-x64-setup.exe` (installer) or `…-win-x64-portable.exe` |
| Windows on ARM | `ParaWerk-0.4.0-alpha.1-win-arm64-setup.exe` or `…-win-arm64-portable.exe` |
| macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `ParaWerk-0.4.0-alpha.1-mac-universal.dmg` (or `…-mac-arm64.dmg` / `…-mac-x64.dmg`) |
| Linux | AppImage, `.deb`, `.rpm`, `.pacman`, Snap, Flatpak or `.tar.gz` for x64 (`x64`, `x86_64`, `amd64`) or arm64 (`arm64`, `aarch64`) |

The builds are not signed with a developer certificate yet, so the system asks once before the first
start (on Windows: *More info → Run anyway*). [Getting started](../user-guide/getting-started.md#install)
shows how to confirm on each system.

## Known limitations

- Not in this alpha yet: multi-body tools, surface tools, sweeps with twist or guide curves, lofts with
  guide curves (Phase 3); assemblies (Phase 4).
- Part files with curves or patterns of patterns (format version 9) cannot be opened by ParaWerk
  0.4.0-alpha.0.

Read the [user guide](../user-guide/README.md) for every tool, step by step.
