# ParaWerk 0.3.0-alpha.1 – Phase 2 complete

This alpha completes the features of **part modeling**, Phase 2 of the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md): thin
features, coordinate systems, fillets and chamfers on whole faces, face fillets and full rounds come on
top of [0.3.0-alpha.0](v0.3.0-alpha.0.md). It is an alpha to try out and give feedback on, not yet a tool
for productive work.

## Highlights

### Thin features

**Thin feature** in Extrude and Revolve makes walls of a *Wall thickness* along the curves of a sketch
instead of the areas they enclose – brackets and channels from a bent profile, ribs from a single line,
tubes from a closed outline, cups from a revolved profile. Open curves work too; a sketch of open curves
starts as a thin feature right away. The walls go to one side, the other or both, lines and arcs stay
exact and meet in sharp corners.

![A bracket extruded as a thin feature from an open profile: flange, bend and web with 3 mm walls](../images/screenshots/extrude-thin.png)

### Rounds

- **Fillets and chamfers on faces:** click a face to round or bevel all of its edges – also edges it
  gets later, such as the rim of a hole cut into it before the fillet.
- **Face fillet:** a round of a radius between two planar faces at a corner, also where they share no
  edge (a chamfer or step between them is rounded over); an inner corner is filled.
- **Full round:** the face between two parallel side faces becomes half a cylinder touching all three –
  rib tops and tab ends.

| Face fillet in an inner corner | Full round on a rib |
|---|---|
| ![Face fillet](../images/screenshots/fillet-faces.png) | ![Full round](../images/screenshots/fillet-full-round.png) |

### Coordinate systems

**Coordinate system** (in the arrow of the **Plane** button) places an origin with three axes: at a
position turned around Z, or at a vertex with its axes along edges. Mass properties can be given
**relative to** it, and exports (STEP, STL, 3MF) are written in its coordinates – for example so that a
part lies on the build plate of a 3D printer the right way up.

![Mass properties relative to a coordinate system on top of the boss](../images/screenshots/mass-properties-csys.png)

### Robustness

The Gate 2 reference suite now has 36 parts with 87 edits at early features; all of them rebuild
without lost references in every CI run on Windows, macOS and Linux.

## Which file do I need?

| System | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (Intel/AMD) | `ParaWerk-0.3.0-alpha.1-win-x64-setup.exe` (installer) or `…-win-x64-portable.exe` |
| Windows on ARM | `ParaWerk-0.3.0-alpha.1-win-arm64-setup.exe` or `…-win-arm64-portable.exe` |
| macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `ParaWerk-0.3.0-alpha.1-mac-universal.dmg` (or `…-mac-arm64.dmg` / `…-mac-x64.dmg`) |
| Linux | AppImage, `.deb`, `.rpm`, `.pacman`, Snap, Flatpak or `.tar.gz` for x64 (`x64`, `x86_64`, `amd64`) or arm64 (`arm64`, `aarch64`) |

The builds are not signed with a developer certificate yet, so the system asks once before the first
start (on Windows: *More info → Run anyway*). [Getting started](../user-guide/getting-started.md#install)
shows how to confirm on each system.

## Known limitations

- Not in this alpha yet: sweep, loft, rib, draft as a feature and configurations (Phase 3); assemblies
  (Phase 4).
- Thin features offset lines, arcs and circles, not ellipses and splines; face fillets work between
  planar faces.
- No multi-body tools yet (combining, splitting or listing separate bodies of a part).
- Part files of 0.3 (format versions 4–7) cannot be opened by ParaWerk 0.2.

Read the [user guide](../user-guide/README.md) for every tool, step by step.
