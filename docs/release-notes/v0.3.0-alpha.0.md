# ParaWerk 0.3.0-alpha.0 – part modeling

This alpha brings **part modeling**, Phase 2 of the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md): sketches become solids,
and real parts can be built from them – plates and housings with bosses, bores, counterbored holes,
patterns, shells, rounds and chamfers. Features keep their faces and edges when earlier features
change, the part can be evaluated (material, mass, measuring, sections), and it goes out as STEP, STL or
3MF. It is an alpha to try out and give feedback on, not yet a tool for productive work.

![A bearing housing built from sketches: plate, boss, bore, counterbored M6 holes, rounds and a chamfer, in aluminium](../images/screenshots/part-modeling.png)

## Highlights

### Extrude and revolve

The closed outlines of a sketch become a solid, or are cut out of the part with *Remove material*.
Outlines inside others become holes, and lines, arcs, circles, ellipses and splines stay exact.

- **Ends:** a depth (also as an equation), *through all*, *up to next* (until each outline meets the
  body, or – cutting – through the first wall it meets), *up to surface* (planar or curved faces and
  planes, with an offset) and *up to vertex*.
- **Directions:** normal, reversed, mid-plane or two directions with their own depths; *draft while
  extruding* tapers the side faces for cast and molded parts.
- **Revolve** turns outlines around a centerline or a reference axis, up to a full turn.

### Holes, patterns, shells, rounds and chamfers

- **Hole:** clearance holes (ISO 273), counterbores for ISO 4762, countersinks for ISO 10642 and tapped
  holes M1.6–M30 at the points of a sketch, through all or to a depth with a drill point; threads are
  shown as dashed circles.
- **Patterns and mirror:** linear and circular patterns and mirror images of extrusions, revolutions,
  holes and primitives.
- **Shell:** hollows the part out, open at picked faces or with a closed cavity.
- **Fillet and chamfer** on edges picked in the 3D view; chamfers also with two distances or a distance
  and an angle.

### Edits that do not break the part

Every face is named after the feature that made it, and fillets and chamfers remember their edges by
the faces they lie between ([ADR 0002](../adr/0002-persistent-naming.md)). A wider sketch, a thicker
plate or a cut inserted before a fillet no longer moves the fillet to other edges; sketches on faces
follow their face. A reference suite of 33 parts is edited at early features in every CI run and must
build cleanly after each of its 78 edits. When an edge is really gone, the fillet keeps the others and
shows where the lost one was.

**Reference geometry** – planes, axes and points built on the part or on each other – carries sketches
and follows the part as well.

### The feature tree

A **rollback bar** builds the part only up to a point, so new features can be inserted in the middle
of the history. Features are **reordered by dragging**; moves that would break what a feature builds on
are refused with an explanation.

![The rollback bar after the boss: later features are greyed out](../images/screenshots/feature-tree-rollback.png)

### Material, mass, measuring and sections

- **Material:** 27 metals, plastics and woods, or your own density, and a color for the part.
- **Mass properties:** mass, volume, surface, center of mass and moments of inertia, exact on the B-Rep.
- **Measure:** distances, angles, center distances, lengths and areas between picked faces, edges and
  vertices, drawn in the 3D view.
- **Section view:** cuts the part open along a plane or face, with filled cut faces.

### Files and exchange

- **STEP import** as a feature: an imported body that later features build on; the file is kept in the
  part.
- **3MF export** for 3D printing, with the part's material and color; STEP and STL export as before.
- **Crash recovery:** unsaved work is copied 15 seconds after each change and offered again after a
  crash or power cut.

### Performance

A part of 301 features (a plate with 100 bosses, each a sketch, an extrusion and a rim fillet) rebuilds
completely in 2.8 s; editing its last feature takes 87 ms (Apple M3). Geometry runs in a background
worker, so the interface never waits for it. More numbers: [benchmarks](../benchmarks/README.md).

## Known limitations

- Not in this alpha yet: thin features, face fillets and full rounds, coordinate systems (rest of
  Phase 2); sweep, loft, rib, draft as a feature and configurations (Phase 3); assemblies (Phase 4).
- No multi-body tools yet (combining, splitting or listing separate bodies of a part).
- Converted model edges in sketches are fixed copies; they do not follow later changes of the model.
- Part files of this version (format versions 4–7) cannot be opened by ParaWerk 0.2.
