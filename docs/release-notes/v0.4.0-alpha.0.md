# ParaWerk 0.4.0-alpha.0 – advanced features and configurations

The first alpha of **Phase 3** of the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md). On top of the part modeling of
[0.3.0-alpha.1](v0.3.0-alpha.1.md), it adds sweeps (also along a helix, for springs), lofts, ribs, drafts,
variable radius fillets, modeled threads, configurations with design tables and full equations. It is an
alpha to try out and give feedback on, not yet a tool for productive work.

## Highlights

### Sweep, helix and loft

**Sweep** (in the arrow of the **Revolve** button) moves the closed outlines of one sketch along a path:
a chain of lines, arcs, ellipses or splines in another sketch – handles, tubes, wires, grooves. The new
**Helix** (in the arrow of the **Plane** button) winds around the circle of a sketch with a pitch, a number
of turns, a start angle and a hand; a sweep along it gives **springs**, coils and helical grooves.

| A handle swept along a bent path | A spring swept along a helix |
|---|---|
| ![Sweep](../images/screenshots/sweep.png) | ![Spring](../images/screenshots/sweep-spring.png) |

**Loft** makes a solid through the outlines of several sketches, in order – from a square duct to a
round one, bottles, blades – smooth or with flat faces between the profiles.

![A transition from a square to a circle 60 mm above it](../images/screenshots/loft.png)

### Rib and draft

- **Rib** turns open sketch lines into a wall up to the body: a gusset in the corner between a floor and
  a wall, within the sketch plane or along its normal. It finds the side with material by itself.
- **Draft** tilts picked faces by an angle about a neutral plane, for molded and cast parts; the faces
  keep their names, so later fillets stay in place.

| Rib in a corner | Draft on the sides of a block |
|---|---|
| ![Rib](../images/screenshots/rib.png) | ![Draft](../images/screenshots/draft.png) |

### Threads and fillets

- **Modeled threads:** click the cylindrical face of a bolt or a hole and get the real groove of the ISO
  metric profile cut along a helix – the size follows the face, the pitch the size. Made for 3D printing;
  for drilled and tapped holes, the hole with a cosmetic thread stays the lighter choice.
- **Variable radius fillets:** the radius changes evenly along each edge from a start to an end value.

![A modeled M10 thread on a bolt](../images/screenshots/thread.png)

### Configurations and equations

**Configurations** keep variants of one part in one file: each has its own values of the part's variables
and its own suppressed features. The **design table** lists them side by side for editing, and a switch in
the feature tree shows another one. Equations now know `min`, `max`, `round`, `floor`, `ceil`,
`asin`, `acos`, `atan` and `if(condition; then; else)` with comparisons; the count of a pattern takes an
equation too.

![Configurations of a part in the design table](../images/screenshots/configurations.png)

### Fixes

- The bounding box in **Mass properties** lies tight around curved faces; it was up to a millimeter too
  large around swept and lofted faces.

## Which file do I need?

| System | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (Intel/AMD) | `ParaWerk-0.4.0-alpha.0-win-x64-setup.exe` (installer) or `…-win-x64-portable.exe` |
| Windows on ARM | `ParaWerk-0.4.0-alpha.0-win-arm64-setup.exe` or `…-win-arm64-portable.exe` |
| macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `ParaWerk-0.4.0-alpha.0-mac-universal.dmg` (or `…-mac-arm64.dmg` / `…-mac-x64.dmg`) |
| Linux | AppImage, `.deb`, `.rpm`, `.pacman`, Snap, Flatpak or `.tar.gz` for x64 (`x64`, `x86_64`, `amd64`) or arm64 (`arm64`, `aarch64`) |

The builds are not signed with a developer certificate yet, so the system asks once before the first
start (on Windows: *More info → Run anyway*). [Getting started](../user-guide/getting-started.md#install)
shows how to confirm on each system.

## Known limitations

- Not in this alpha yet: multi-body tools, projected and composite curves, surface tools, sweeps with
  twist or guide curves (Phase 3); assemblies (Phase 4).
- Along a helix, a sweep takes outlines without holes; a spring of many turns takes a second or more to
  build and show.
- Part files with the features of 0.4 (format version 8) cannot be opened by ParaWerk 0.3; it reports a
  newer file.

Read the [user guide](../user-guide/README.md) for every tool, step by step.
