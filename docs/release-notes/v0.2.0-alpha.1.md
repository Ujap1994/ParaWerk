# ParaWerk 0.2.0-alpha.1 – the complete 2D sketcher

This preview completes the **2D sketcher**, Phase 1 of the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md). New since
0.2.0-alpha.0: splines that points can be held on and that trim exactly, converting edges of the part
into a sketch, scaling, and variables and equations for the whole part. Turning sketches into solids
(extrude, revolve) is the next step in Phase 2, so this is still a preview to try out, not a tool for
real parts.

![A profile with a spline between two lines and a point held on it](../images/screenshots/sketch-spline.png)

## Highlights

### Splines

A spline (key **S**) is a smooth curve through the points you click: a natural cubic spline that passes
through every fit point and runs straight out at both ends. The fit points are ordinary sketch points, so
they snap, take relations and dimensions and can be dragged, and the curve follows.

- **Points on splines:** points snap onto the curve between the fit points and stay there. They slide
  along the spline until a dimension holds them and move along when the fit points move.
- **Trim and extend at splines:** lines, arcs and circles are trimmed where a spline crosses them and
  extended up to it; their new ends stay on the spline.
- **Exact cutting:** a spline cut at another curve keeps all of its fit points, so its shape does not
  change at all. While the sketch is open, the cut-off part stays visible as a faint dashed hint, because
  its fit points still shape the curve.

### Convert model edges

**Convert** takes edges of the part into the sketch: lines stay lines, circles and arcs stay circles and
arcs, tilted circles become ellipses and other curves become splines. Touching edges share their end
points, so a face outline becomes a closed profile right away.

![Edges of the box top and the circle of the boss converted into the sketch](../images/screenshots/sketch-convert.png)

### Variables and equations

**Variables** (in the *Features* tab) holds named values for the whole part, also as equations over each
other (for example `depth` as `width / 2`). Type a name or an equation into any parameter or sketch
dimension, and it follows the variable; change the variable, and the part rebuilds in one undo step.
Every number field also calculates: `120/3`, `2*pi*5`, `1.5in`, `sqrt(2)*10`.

### More

- **Scale** the selection around the origin or a selected point, in place or as a scaled copy.
- **Points before symbols:** a sketch point right under the cursor now wins over a relation symbol, so
  points stay easy to grab in a zoomed-out view.
- Part files that use variables, equations or splines are saved as format version 3; older versions of
  ParaWerk cannot open them.

| System | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (Intel/AMD) | `ParaWerk-0.2.0-alpha.1-win-x64-setup.exe` (installer) or `…-win-x64-portable.exe` |
| Windows on ARM | `ParaWerk-0.2.0-alpha.1-win-arm64-setup.exe` or `…-win-arm64-portable.exe` |
| macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `ParaWerk-0.2.0-alpha.1-mac-universal.dmg` (or `…-mac-arm64.dmg` / `…-mac-x64.dmg`) |
| Linux | AppImage, `.deb`, `.rpm`, `.pacman`, Snap, Flatpak or `.tar.gz` for x64 (`x64`, `x86_64`, `amd64`) or arm64 (`arm64`, `aarch64`) |

The builds are not signed with a developer certificate yet, so the system asks once before the first
start. [Getting started](../user-guide/getting-started.md#install) shows how to confirm on each system.

## Known limitations

- Sketches cannot be extruded or revolved yet (Phase 2).
- Converted model edges are fixed copies; they do not follow later changes of the model yet.
- Ellipses can only be trimmed as a whole; offset leaves ellipses and splines out.
- DXF import skips splines given only by control points, elliptical arcs, texts, dimensions, hatches and
  blocks (a message lists them).
